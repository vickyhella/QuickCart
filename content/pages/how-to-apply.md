Press-ons should feel simple. Set aside a few minutes, lay out your kit, and work on clean, dry nails.

![Four photo examples: clean your natural nail, dry-fit a press-on, add adhesive, and press into place](/site/guides/how-to-apply.svg)

## 1. Prep your natural nails

Wash and dry your hands. Gently push back the cuticles with the included stick, lightly buff the surface of each natural nail, brush away dust, then wipe each nail with an alcohol pad. Let the nails dry fully before continuing.

## 2. Find the right fit

Before using adhesive, try each press-on against the matching natural nail. Choose a tip that covers the nail from sidewall to sidewall without sitting on your skin. If you are between sizes, choose the tip that fits the nail bed best; gently file the sides only if needed. Lay the tips out in order so they are easy to pick up.

Need help measuring? See the [Nail Size Guide](/size-guide/).

## 3. Choose one adhesive method

Use either the included jelly adhesive pieces or nail glue. Follow the directions on the adhesive packaging and apply only to the natural nail and/or press-on as directed. Keep adhesive off skin, and do not combine the two methods on the same nail.

## 4. Place and press

Line up the press-on near the cuticle without covering the skin, then lower it onto the nail. Press firmly from the base toward the tip and hold for the time stated on the adhesive packaging. Repeat one nail at a time.

### A few helpful reminders

- Keep water, lotion, and cuticle oil away during prep and application.
- Avoid applying over irritated or damaged nails or skin.
- If you feel discomfort or notice irritation, remove the set and stop using it.
- Adhesive wear and removal vary; follow the instructions supplied with your adhesive.
