Ginny Nana values your privacy. This policy explains how we use information provided through our store. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

## Information we collect and use

When you place an order, we collect your email, recipient name, shipping address, optional phone number and order notes, purchased items, and order and payment status. We use this information to process payments, fulfill orders, provide tracking and support, and maintain order records.

If you use a customer account, we process your account and sign-in information to authenticate you and show your order history. Messages sent through our contact form include your name, email, subject and message so we can respond.

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we record your email address, consent time and subscription source. Contact us by email if you want to withdraw your subscription.

## Payments and service providers

Payments are processed by PayPal. When card payment is offered, card details are entered through PayPal's payment fields. Our store does not store your full card number or card security code. We retain payment references and payment status needed to manage your order.

We share information needed to operate the store with service providers, such as payment, hosting, data storage, order email and delivery providers. They receive information needed for those services. This operational sharing is different from selling personal data.

## Cookies and browser storage

We use a sign-in cookie for authenticated customer sessions and browser local storage to remember your shopping cart. Clearing browser storage may remove your saved cart; clearing the sign-in cookie signs you out. PayPal may use its own cookies and process information according to its privacy policy when you use its services.

## Your questions and requests

For questions about your information or requests to access, correct or delete it, email [ginnynana@hotmail.com](mailto:ginnynana@hotmail.com). Some order and transaction records may need to be retained to meet applicable obligations.
