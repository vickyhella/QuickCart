## Tracking and assistance

When your order is marked as shipped, its tracking details can be viewed on the [Order status](/my-orders/) page or in [your account](/account/). You can look up an order using its order number and checkout email. Shipping notifications include tracking details when available.

For questions about delivery or an order that has not arrived, email [ginnynana@hotmail.com](mailto:ginnynana@hotmail.com) with your order number. For damaged or incorrect items, see our [Refunds & Returns policy](/refunds-returns/).
