Press-on sizing is based on the width of each natural nail. Measure across the widest part of the nail plate, from sidewall to sidewall.

## How to measure

**With flexible tape:** Place the tape across the widest point of the nail, following its curve from one sidewall to the other. Note the millimeter mark.

**With paper:** Place a narrow strip across the nail, mark both sidewalls, then lay it flat and measure the distance between the marks with a millimeter ruler.

Measure both hands: matching fingers can have different widths. If a measurement falls between sizes, compare the actual tip against your nail before applying adhesive. For a closer fit, a press-on can be gently filed at its side edges.
